## 1. The extension

UniLit is a non-custodial wallet. The seed phrase and the private keys stay on your device, encrypted with the password you choose. UniLit does not have the key, does not hold your LTC, and cannot move funds, freeze funds, or reverse a Litecoin transaction.

You may use the extension to create or restore a wallet, view balances and history, connect to a site you approve, and review and sign transactions. A feature may be unavailable on a given network.

## 2. Third-party services

The extension asks other services for data so it can function. Your use of those services is also subject to their own terms.

- Balances and history are requested from [litecoinspace.org](https://litecoinspace.org), or from an explorer URL you set.
- Litecoin content and previews are requested from [litescribe.io](https://litescribe.io).
- The LTC-USD price is requested from Coinbase, then from Kraken if Coinbase does not answer.
- Phishing warnings use a public blocklist published by MetaMask.
- Feedback opens a GitHub issue.

UniLit does not control those services. They can be wrong, slow, or unavailable. A balance, price, inscription, or phishing result shown in the extension can be incomplete. Relying on it is your decision.

Sites you connect to, and hardware wallets you use, are also third parties. Approving a connection gives that site the account you approve. It does not give the site your seed phrase. What the site does with the account is between you and that site.
